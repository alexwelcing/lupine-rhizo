TMS 2027 digest — Lupine Science MLIP validation program (brief asOf 2026-09-09)
WHAT WE DO: benchmark-driven validation of universal interatomic potentials (UIPs), mapping where they actually work via Matbench Discovery (2026-09-09 snapshot) plus community failure benchmarks, feeding error-geometry and validation-gate methods.
NEGATIVE 1: discovery F1 has saturated (best 0.931 vs DAF ceiling ~6.54) — the frontier is phonons, geometry robustness, and MD stability; discovery rankings alone are spent.
NEGATIVE 2: training data, not architecture, is the lever — all top-10 models are OAM-mix fits; identical architectures drop 6-16 CPS points in MPtrj-only variants.
NEGATIVE 3: the 2024 "household" universals are mid-table or worse — MACE-MPA-0 21st, MatterSim 24th, MACE-MP-0 34th, M3GNet 40th, CHGNet 41st.
NEGATIVE 4: universals still fail on amorphous materials, zeolites, supported-nanoparticle exploration, and long-range electrostatics/magnetism/charge transfer.
